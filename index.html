THE FIELD GUIDE

Lead the initiative.
Read the room.

Nothing moves until you do. Each action gives the risks one turn.

  1. Move or resolve. Tap a neighboring hex to move. Tap a neighboring risk to resolve 1 point. Resolving a risk earns 1 influence and leaves an artifact; collecting it earns 1 more.
  2. Read the intentions. Striped hexes will be disrupted next turn. Numbers show overlapping threats. Arrows show a risk’s planned move. Choose Inspect a risk, then tap it on the board. Inspecting costs no turn.
  3. Earn tools as you go. Level 1 teaches movement and resolution. At each promotion, you unlock one tool. Tap a tool to preview it, then choose a highlighted target. Facilitate and Recovery plan show a Use button before spending influence. Waiting restores 1 influence but risks still act.
  4. Reach the gate. Resolve the required number of risks, then move onto the diamond gate. Promotions restore 3 capacity, refill influence, and unlock a tool. From the second promotion onward, also choose an upgrade.

Keyboard: Tab and Enter work throughout. On the board, arrows browse hexes; Enter selects. Key 1 returns to movement; keys 2–6 select unlocked tools. W waits from Level 2 onward. Escape cancels a selected capability.

Sound is optional. This demo sends no analytics or personal information. “Talk with TGG” opens TGG’s website.
